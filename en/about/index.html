BenderChat — a long-running project

BenderChat is a sandbox MMORPG that combines building, exploration, RPG progression and clan warfare.

The team states that the project has been worked on continuously since 2012. This site is organized to make the client, registration, server status, race statistics and community channels easy to find.

Project mascot — Bender

Explore a huge world, build, fight, grow your character and team up with clans. BenderChat blends sandbox freedom with MMO RPG progression.
